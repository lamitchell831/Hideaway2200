# Tesla Influencer Content Pipeline

## Daily Sources to Monitor
- @elonmusk (primary)
- @Tesla
- Electrek.co
- InsideEVs
- Teslarati
- Drive Tesla Canada
- Not a Tesla App
- X EV community (search: "Tesla" + "news" + filter: today)

## Content Types
1. **News Reaction** (40%) — Breaking Tesla/EV news + your hot take
2. **Analysis** (30%) — Deep dives on deliveries, FSD, margins
3. **Memes/Shitposts** (20%) — Humor, engagement bait
4. **Thread Posts** (10%) — Long-form storytelling

## Posting Schedule
- Morning (8-9 AM ET): Overnight news recap
- Lunch (12-1 PM): Analysis/Opinion
- Afternoon (3-4 PM): Breaking news/reaction
- Evening (7-9 PM): Engagement (replies, memes)

## Hooks That Work
- "Tesla just did something nobody noticed..."
- "Here's why [news] matters for [specific audience]"
- "Unpopular opinion: [controversial take]"
- "Thread: 5 things [topic]"

## Engagement Targets
- Reply to @elonmusk within 5 minutes of his posts
- Reply to top Tesla accounts daily (@WholeMarsBlog, @SawyerMerritt, etc.)
- Quote tweet viral EV posts with unique angle

## Metrics to Track
- Follower growth (weekly)
- Impressions per post (aim for 10K+ average)
- Engagement rate (target 2%+)
- Profile visits → Follow conversion
