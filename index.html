Your Mountain Retreat

Asheville Tiny House with Private Pond

Hideaway 2200 is a modern tiny house rental in Asheville, NC that defies expectations. Nestled beside a private pond just minutes from downtown, this 400-square-foot retreat sleeps up to 8 guests comfortably — perfect for families or friend groups seeking a unique mountain getaway.

Unlike cramped hotel rooms, you'll enjoy a full kitchen with dishwasher and full-size fridge, a real bathroom (not a wet bath!), and a large porch overlooking the water. End your days gathered around the fire pit as the stars come out over the Blue Ridge Mountains.
