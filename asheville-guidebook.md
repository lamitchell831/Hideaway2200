## 💡 LOCAL TIPS

### Getting Around
- **Parking Downtown** — Street meters (free after 6 PM), garages
- **ART Bus** — Free downtown circulator
- **Uber/Lyft** — Readily available
- **E-bike rentals** — Great way to see the city
- **Walking** — Downtown is very walkable

### Best Times to Visit
- **Spring (Apr-May)** — Rhododendrons, waterfalls gushing
- **Fall (Sep-Oct)** — Leaf peeping, peak crowds
- **Summer** — Cooler than lowlands, afternoon storms
- **Winter** — Quiet, Biltmore candlelight tours

### Weather Notes
- **"Elevation is everything"** — Can be 10-15° cooler than nearby cities
- **Pack layers** — Mountain weather changes fast
- **Afternoon thunderstorms** — Common in summer

### Asheville Etiquette
- **Shop local** — Support the maker culture
- **Be patient** — It's a small city; service is friendly but not fast
- **Respect the vibe** — It's crunchy, laid-back, artsy
- **Leave no trace** — Pack out trash on trails
